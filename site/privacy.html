Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Rightroom is a partnership intelligence tool for founders, built by Somehow Internet. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it's kept and what you can do about it.

What we collect

WhenWhatWhy
You request accessName, email, company website, X handle (optional) and the partnership you're looking forTo decide who joins the invite-only pilot and to reply to you
You sign inYour name, and your email or X handle, from the account you sign in with (X, Google or an email code), plus an account ID from our sign-in providerTo recognise you and check you were invited
You use the appYour onboarding answers (role, company, what you build, who buys it, goals, markets), searches, saved companies, pipeline stages, follow-up dates, notes and draftsTo rank companies for you and keep your work, in your private workspace
Every visitOne sign-in cookie (below) and standard technical logs kept by our hosting providersTo keep you signed in and keep the service secure

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell or rent personal information. Signing in with X or Google only shares your name and email or handle. We don't read your email, contacts or posts.

Cookies

We use one cookie, __Host-rr_session, to keep you signed in for up to 30 days. It's essential, so there's no cookie banner. Our sign-in provider, Privy, may keep a small amount of data in your browser to complete sign-in. Our fonts are hosted by us, not loaded from other sites.

Who processes data for us

  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare runs the app's back end and the database where your data is stored.
  • Privy handles sign-in (X, Google and email codes).

These providers may process data in other countries. Rightroom doesn't send your workspace data to any AI service. If that ever changes, we'll ask you first.

Companies and people in Rightroom

Rightroom's company database is built from public sources: company websites, announcements, documentation, public partner pages and permitted APIs. Each fact links to its source. We only show a named person when a public page shows them in that role, with the page and the date we checked it. We never guess email addresses.

If you're named in Rightroom and want a correction or removal, tell us (see “Contact” below) and we'll act within 30 days.

How long we keep data

  • Your account and workspace: until you delete your account. In the app, go to Your company → Delete my account and data. This immediately removes your account and everything in your workspace. Your original access request and your sign-in record at Privy are removed on request.
  • Access requests: deleted automatically 12 months after we decide on them, or sooner on request.
  • Technical logs: kept for the hosting providers' standard periods.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal information, and object to how we use it. Founders can delete their account and data in the app at any time. You can also complain to your data protection authority, such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Contact

We don't have a dedicated contact email yet; one will be added here before public launch. Until then, send privacy requests through the Request access form and start your message with “Privacy request”.

Children

Rightroom is for founders and their teams, and isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If a change matters, we'll let founders know before it applies.
